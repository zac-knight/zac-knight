## Selected work

### 🌾 Cotton Knowledge Hub
*For the Cotton Research and Development Corporation (CRDC) · University industry project · 2026 to present · Team of six, which I lead*

A chat style assistant that answers questions about cotton farming research and links each answer to the exact paragraph it came from. It uses a technique called retrieval augmented generation (RAG): the AI first searches a library of documents, then writes its answer from what it found. The library holds nearly 2,000 research reports from 1986 to 2025. CRDC is an Australian organisation that invests in research for the cotton industry.

What our team did:

* Worked out what the system should do and wrote it as a backlog of **240 user stories**, which are short descriptions of a feature from the user's point of view.
* Wrote **1,796 automated tests**, which found **101 bugs**.
* Mapped how the system finds and reads documents. We estimate one checking step uses about **86%** of the AI's reading effort on every question, and we proposed a redesign that cuts it from about 40 AI calls per question to 3 or 4.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Postgres and pgvector` `Supabase` `OpenAI` `Next.js` `Google Cloud Run`

### 🚓 Post Course Resource Hub
*For the Queensland Police Service (QPS) · Final year university capstone · 2024 · Team of four*

A website where detectives and specialist investigators keep learning after they finish a training course. It has course pages, discussion forums, a research library and a shop.

Our team planned and built it from scratch, delivering **110 user stories** over six months. Alongside the coding I wrote user guides, recorded a 56 minute demo and presented to the client every fortnight.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `React` `MySQL` `Prisma` `Tailwind CSS`
