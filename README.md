## Selected work

### Cotton Knowledge Hub, for CRDC
*University industry project · 2026 to present · team of six, which I lead*

**What it is:** A chat style assistant that answers questions about cotton farming research and links each answer to the exact paragraph it came from. It uses a technique called retrieval augmented generation (RAG), where the AI first searches a library of documents and then writes its answer from what it found. This library holds nearly 2,000 research reports from 1986 to 2025.

**What our team did:**
* Worked out what the system should do and wrote it up as a backlog of 240 user stories, which are short descriptions of a feature from the user's point of view.
* Wrote 1,796 automated tests, which found 101 bugs.
* Mapped how the system finds and reads documents, and estimated that one checking step accounts for about 86% of the AI's reading effort on every question. We proposed a redesign that would cut that step from about 40 AI calls per question to 3 or 4.

`Python` `FastAPI` `Postgres and pgvector` `Supabase` `OpenAI` `Next.js` `Google Cloud Run`

### Post Course Resource Hub, for the Queensland Police Service (QPS)
*Final year university capstone · 2024 · team of four*

**What it is:** A website where detectives and specialist investigators keep learning after they finish a training course. It has course pages, discussion forums, a research library and a shop.

**What I did:** Our team planned and built it from scratch, delivering 110 user stories over six months. Alongside the coding I wrote user guides, recorded a 56 minute demo and presented to the client every fortnight.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `MySQL` `Prisma` `Tailwind CSS` `React`
